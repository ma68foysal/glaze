# Demo store images — Kinetic Editorial

Free photos from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license), selected with the
site's free-licence filter (no Unsplash+ photos). The Unsplash licence allows
commercial use without attribution. Credit is still recorded here.

These images are for the **demo store only**. They live in the store's Files
and are referenced from the demo theme's settings on the store. They must
never be committed into `templates/*.json` or `config/settings_data.json`:
`shopify://shop_images/...` references break on a merchant's install and are a
Theme Store rejection reason.

**Palette rule:** ink, paper and stone, with black, camel, grey and denim
clothing. No saturated colour backgrounds. Motion, city streets, workshop and
fabric texture.
